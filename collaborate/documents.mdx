---
title: "Store and find documents"
description: "Upload, organize, and share your company's documents in one drive."
---

**Documents** is one drive for every entity's files. You can upload files, organize them into folders, and share them with a link.

## Find a document

1. In the sidebar, select **Documents**.
2. Open **All Files** and browse the folders. To search every entity at once, use **Search documents across all entities**.
3. Open a file's actions and select **Preview** to view it or **Download** to save it.

Select **View details** to see a file's **Properties**, such as its **Document category**, **Tax type**, **Jurisdiction**, and **Period**.

## Upload documents

1. In **Documents**, open the folder you want.
2. Select **Upload**, then choose **Upload files** or **Upload folder**.

To make a new folder first, select **New folder**.

## Organize files

From a file's actions you can:

- **Rename** the file.
- **Move to...** another folder.
- **Trash** the file.

To act on several files at once, select them and use **Move** or **Move to trash**.

## Share files

1. Select the files you want to share.
2. Select **Create share link**.

Your links are listed under **Shared Links**.

## Restore a file from trash

1. In the Documents sidebar, open **Trash**.
2. Select the file and choose **Restore**.

To remove it for good, select **Permanently delete**.
