---
title: "Message your Commenda team"
description: "Use Inbox to talk to your account manager and follow conversations about your services and filings."
---

**Inbox** collects every conversation you have with Commenda. Each service and filing has its own thread, so messages stay with the work they're about.

## Read your messages

1. In the sidebar, select **Inbox**. The sidebar shows a dot when you have unread messages.
2. Choose **Unread** or **All**, or use **Filter threads**.
3. Select a conversation to see the full thread.

Pinned conversations stay at the top under **Pinned**. To clear your unread messages, open **Inbox options** and select **Mark all as read**.

## Message your account manager

Your account manager is your main contact at Commenda.

1. In **Inbox**, open **Inbox options**.
2. Select **Message your account manager**.
3. Write your message and send it.

## Reply about a service or filing

1. Open the conversation for the service or filing.
2. Write your reply and send it.

To see the service a thread belongs to, select **Go to service**.
