---
title: "Track deadlines in the Compliance Calendar"
description: "See every filing deadline across your entities, and add filings Commenda doesn't track yet."
---

The **Compliance Calendar** shows every filing due across your entities. You see what's due, what's overdue, and who's working on it.

## Set up the calendar

Commenda builds the calendar from your entity details. The first time you open **Compliance Calendar**, you complete compliance onboarding for an entity.

1. In the sidebar, select **Compliance Calendar**.
2. Select **Get started**.
3. Review each step: **General Business Information**, **Entity Details**, **Locations**, **Tax & Registrations**, and **Review and Confirmation**.
4. Select **Confirm & submit**. To finish later, select **Save & exit**.

Commenda shows **Generating compliance calendar** while it works out your filings.

## Read the calendar

- Switch between the **Month** and **Agenda** views. Use **Today**, **Previous**, and **Next** to move through time.
- Under **My Entities**, choose which entities to show. Use **Show all** or **Hide all** to toggle them together.
- In the list view, filter filings by **Upcoming**, **Overdue**, **Completed**, or **Archived**.

Select a filing to open its details, including its **Period**, **Due date**, **Status**, and **Assigned to**.

## Add a filing

Add a filing when you know about an obligation that the calendar doesn't show.

1. Select **Add filing**.
2. Choose the **Entity**.
3. Search for the filing name, or select **Create new filing** and enter a **Filing name**.
4. Choose the **Filing type** and **Filing frequency**, and set the **First due date**.
5. Select **Add filing**.

## Act on a filing

Open a filing and use **Filing actions**:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Manage assignees** | Choose the team members responsible for the filing. |
| **Extend filing** | Set an **Extended due date** and upload the extension document. |
| **Upload receipt** | Attach proof that the filing was submitted. |
| **Archive filing** | Hide a filing that doesn't apply. Commenda asks you why. Use **Unarchive** to restore it. |

Indirect tax filings live in Global Indirect Tax. See [Review your filings](/indirect-tax/filings).
