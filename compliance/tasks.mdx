---
title: "Complete your tasks"
description: "Find and finish the tasks Commenda needs from you."
---

**Tasks** lists everything Commenda needs from you to move a service or filing forward. The sidebar shows a count when you have tasks pending.

## Find your tasks

1. In the sidebar, select **Tasks**.
2. Use **Search for task** to find a task by name, or group the list with **Group**.

Each task shows its **Task name**, **Service**, **Entity**, and **Status**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Started** | Nobody has worked on it yet. |
| **In Progress** | Work has started. |
| **Action Required** | Commenda is waiting on you. |

## Complete a task

1. Select the task. Commenda opens the service or filing it belongs to.
2. Follow the steps on that page, such as answering questions or uploading a document.

The task status updates as the work moves forward.
