---
title: "Add and manage entities"
description: "Add a legal entity and keep its details, bank accounts, and documents up to date."
---

Each legal entity you add gets its own page in Commenda. Commenda uses that page to work out the entity's compliance and tax obligations, so keep it accurate.

## Add an entity

1. In the sidebar, select **Legal Entities**.
2. Select **Add entity**.
3. Enter the **Country**, **Company legal name**, **Date of incorporation**, **Jurisdiction**, **Entity type**, and **Fiscal year end**.
4. Select **Create entity**.

The new entity appears in the list on **Legal Entities**.

## Find an entity

**Legal Entities** lists every entity with its **Entity name**, **Status**, **Jurisdiction**, and **Date of Formation**.

- Use **Search entities** to filter the list.
- Switch to **Org Chart** to see how your entities relate to each other, or to **Tax Chart** for the tax view.
- Select an entity to open its page.

## Update an entity's details

An entity's page has these tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Information** | The entity's details, split into **General**, **Tax**, **Registrations**, **Locations**, and **Credentials**. |
| **Bank Accounts** | The entity's bank accounts. |
| **Ownership** | Shareholders, share classes, and ultimate beneficial owners. See [Record ownership](/entities/ownership). |
| **Entity Documents** | Files stored for this entity. |
| **Key Persons** | The people who hold roles in this entity. See [Track key persons](/entities/key-persons). |
| **Virtual Mailroom** | Mail received at the entity's registered address. This tab shows only for entities that support it. |
| **My Services** | The Commenda services you have for this entity. |
| **Settings** | Entity settings, including **Delete entity**. |

To change a field, open the tab and select **Edit**.

## Store portal credentials

Commenda needs your tax portal logins to file for you. Add them on the **Business Information** tab.

1. Open **Credentials** and select **Add credential**.
2. Choose the **Credential type**, **Country**, and **Jurisdiction**.
3. Enter the **Login ID** and **Password**. Add **Notes** if your team needs them.
4. To link the credential to a registration, choose it in **Linked Registration (optional)**.
5. Save the credential.

## Delete an entity

1. Open the entity and select the **Settings** tab.
2. Select **Delete entity** and confirm.

<Warning>Deleting an entity can't be undone.</Warning>
