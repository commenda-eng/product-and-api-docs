---
title: "Track key persons"
description: "Record the directors, officers, and other people who hold roles in your entities."
---

A key person is someone who plays a role in one or more of your entities, such as a director. **Key Persons** keeps one profile per person, with their roles across all your entities.

## Add a key person

1. In the sidebar, select **Key Persons**.
2. Select **Add key person**.
3. Fill in the person's details:
   - **Identity**: **Full Legal Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Gender**, **Nationalities**, **Country of Citizenship**, and **Country of Residence**.
   - **Contact Information**: **Primary Email**, **Primary Phone**, and **Residential Address**.
4. Save the person. Commenda opens their profile.

## Assign a role

Roles connect a person to an entity.

1. Open the person's profile.
2. In **Roles**, select **Assign role(s)**.
3. Choose the **Entity** and the **Role**, then save.

The role appears under **Active roles**.

## End a role

When someone leaves a role, end it instead of deleting it. Commenda keeps the history.

1. In **Roles**, open the role's actions and select **End role**.
2. Enter the **Removal date** and confirm.

## Add proof of identity

1. On the person's profile, go to **Proof of Identity** and select **Add**.
2. Choose the **Country** and **Document type**, and enter the document details.
3. Save the document.

Each document shows **Valid**, **Expiring soon**, or **Expired** so you know when to replace it.

## Remove a key person

On **Key Persons**, open the person's actions and select **Remove key person**.
