---
title: "Record ownership"
description: "Record who owns an entity and who its ultimate beneficial owners are."
---

The **Ownership** tab shows who holds shares in an entity and who controls it. Commenda uses it to build your org chart and to flag ownership that adds up to more than 100%.

## Open the Ownership tab

1. In the sidebar, select **Legal Entities**.
2. Open the entity and select **Ownership**.

**Ownership distribution** at the top summarizes the stakes you've recorded. Some entity types don't record ownership. For those, the tab says so.

## Add a shareholder

1. Select **Add** and choose **Individual** or **Entity**.
2. For an individual, choose an existing person or select **Add person** and enter their details.
3. For an entity, choose **Link to existing entity in your account** or **Add new entity**.
4. Enter the stake and save.

## Edit share classes

Share classes are types of shares, such as **Common** or **Preferred**.

1. On the **Ownership** tab, select **Edit share classes**.
2. Enter a **Class name**, choose a **Type**, and set the number of shares.
3. Save your changes.

## Record ultimate beneficial owners

An ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) is a person who ultimately owns or controls the entity.

1. Under **Ultimate Beneficial Owners**, select **Add**.
2. Choose the person and their **Control type**.
3. Save.

To change how a UBO controls the entity, select **Edit control type**. To end a UBO's role, select **End UBO role** and enter the **Removal date**.

## Upload ownership documents

1. Select **Upload ownership documents**.
2. Choose **Upload files** or **Upload folder**.

The files appear under **Recent documents** and in **Documents**.
