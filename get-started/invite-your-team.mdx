---
title: "Invite your team"
description: "Give a colleague access to Commenda and choose their role."
---

When you invite someone, they get an email invite to join your company in Commenda.

<Note>Only admins can invite and remove users.</Note>

## Invite a user

1. Open **Settings** and select **Team and roles**.
2. Select **Invite user**.
3. Enter the person's **Name** and **Email address**.
4. Choose a **Role**.
5. Select **Send invite**.

## Choose a role

Every user has one of two roles.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything a user can do, plus invite and remove users. |
| **User** | Everything else in Commenda. |

To change a role, open the person's actions in **Team and roles** and select **Edit role**. Choose the new role and select **Save changes**.

## Remove a user

1. In **Team and roles**, open the person's actions.
2. Select **Remove user**.
3. Confirm in the **Remove team member** dialog.
