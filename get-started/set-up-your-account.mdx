When you finish setup, your company and its entities are in Commenda, and you can start using the Compliance Calendar and Global Indirect Tax.

The first time you sign in, Commenda walks you through two short steps. You can't skip them.

## Add your details

1. On **Let's get to know you**, enter your name, your role, and your phone number.
2. Select **Next**.

## Add your company details

1. On **Tell us about your company**, enter your company's name, where it's headquartered, and its website.
2. Select **Next**.

## Add your entities

An entity is a company you own or manage, such as a subsidiary in another country. Add each one so Commenda can track its obligations.

1. On **Onboard your entities**, select **Add entity**.
2. Enter the entity's details:
   - **Country**
   - **Company legal name**
   - **Date of incorporation**
   - **Jurisdiction**, if the country has one
   - **Entity type**
   - **Fiscal year end**
3. Select **Create entity**.
4. Repeat for each entity. The entities you add appear under **Entities added**.

You can add more entities later from **Legal Entities**. See [Add and manage entities](/entities/add-and-manage-entities).
