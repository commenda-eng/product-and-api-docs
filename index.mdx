## Find your way around

The left sidebar in the app groups everything you can do.

| Sidebar item | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See what needs your attention. |
| **Tasks** | Complete the tasks Commenda needs from you. |
| **Inbox** | Message your Commenda team about services and filings. |
| **Legal Entities** | Add entities and keep their details up to date. |
| **Key Persons** | Track the people who hold roles in your entities. |
| **Compliance Calendar** | See every filing deadline across your entities. |
| **Documents** | Store and find files for each entity. |
| **Global Indirect Tax** | Track exposure, registrations, and filings for sales tax, VAT, and GST. |

Your profile, team, integrations, and billing live in **Settings**.
