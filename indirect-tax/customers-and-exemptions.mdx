---
title: "Manage customers and exemption certificates"
description: "Keep your customer list current and collect exemption certificates so exempt sales aren't taxed."
---

Some customers don't pay tax, for example resellers or nonprofits. They prove it with an exemption certificate. When a customer has an approved certificate for a state, Commenda doesn't tax their sales there.

<Note>Exemption certificates apply only to future transactions. They can't be applied retroactively.</Note>

## Add customers

Commenda adds customers from your transactions and connected systems. To add one yourself:

1. In **Global Indirect Tax**, select **Customers**.
2. Select **Add customer**.
3. Enter the **Customer name** and **Email**, and save.

To add many at once, select **Import customers**, upload your file, and map its columns in **Map CSV columns to customers table**.

## Ask a customer for a certificate

1. Find the customer in **Customers**.
2. Select **Request exemption**.
3. Confirm **Send exemption certificate request**.

Commenda emails the customer. **Last emailed** shows when the request went out.

## Add a certificate yourself

1. In **Customers**, open the customer.
2. Select **Add exemption**.
3. Choose the customer, the **Certificate type**, the states, and the **Exemption reason**. Add the **Expiration date** if there is one.
4. Upload the certificate document.
5. Save.

## Review a certificate

Certificates your customers submit wait for your review.

1. Open the certificate.
2. Check the details against the document.
3. Select **Approve**, or select **Reject** and give a reason so the buyer can fix it.

Each certificate shows its **Review status** and **Expiration date**.
