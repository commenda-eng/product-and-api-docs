---
title: "Track your exposure"
description: "See where your sales cross a registration threshold and start a registration."
---

**Exposure Tracking** shows, for each jurisdiction, whether your sales have crossed the threshold that requires you to register. You use it to decide where to register next.

## Read your exposure

1. In **Global Indirect Tax**, select **Exposure Tracking**.
2. Choose the entity at the top of the page.

Each row is a jurisdiction. Filter by **Nexus status**:

| Nexus status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Exposed** | Your activity crossed the threshold. You need to register. |
| **Approaching** | Your activity is close to the threshold. |
| **Not exposed** | Your activity is below the threshold. |

**Registration status** shows where you are with that jurisdiction: **Not registered**, **Registration started**, **Validation in progress**, or **Remitting**. **Total sales after exposure** shows the sales you made after you crossed the threshold.

If the list is empty, upload transactions first. See [Import your transactions](/indirect-tax/transactions).

## See why you're exposed

Select a jurisdiction to see the details:

- **Threshold type**: whether the threshold counts sales, transactions, or both.
- **Evaluation period**: the time window Commenda measures.
- **Exclusions**: sale and transaction types the threshold doesn't count.
- **Exposure date**: when your activity first crossed the threshold.

To leave context for your team, select **Add note**.

## Start a registration

1. Find a jurisdiction where you're exposed.
2. Select **Register**. If you already started, select **Continue registration**.

Commenda opens the registration as a service, so you can track it and message your team about it. See [Manage your registrations](/indirect-tax/registrations).
