---
title: "Review your filings"
description: "See what's due for each registration, and check a return before it's filed."
---

**Filings** lists every indirect tax return for an entity. Commenda creates filings from your registrations and fills them from your transactions.

## See your filings

1. In **Global Indirect Tax**, select **Filings**.
2. Use **Search filings** or filter the list.

Each row shows the **Filing**, **Jurisdiction**, **Tax period**, **Due date**, and **Status**.

If the list is empty, you don't have a registration yet. Select **Go to registrations** to add one.

## Check a filing

Select a filing to open it.

- **Summary** shows the **Return summary**, such as gross, taxable, non-taxable, and exempt sales.
- **Filing details** shows the registration, the portal, and the remittance bank account.
- **Activity** shows what's happened on the filing.

To see the transactions included in the return, open **Filing transactions**.

To see tax due by jurisdiction, select **View tax liability report**. Select **Export CSV** to download it.

## Report a problem

If something in a filing looks wrong, tell Commenda before it's filed.

1. Open the filing.
2. Select **Report issue**.
3. Describe what needs to change and send it.
