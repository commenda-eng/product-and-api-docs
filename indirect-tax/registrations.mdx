---
title: "Manage your registrations"
description: "Add the tax registrations you already have, or ask Commenda to get a new one."
---

A registration is your account with a tax authority in one jurisdiction. Commenda uses your registrations to calculate tax and to prepare your filings.

## See your registrations

In **Global Indirect Tax**, select **Registrations**. Each row shows the **Registration name**, **Status**, **Validation status**, **Filing frequency**, **Tax calculation**, **Managed filing**, and **Effective date**.

## Add a registration you already have

1. In **Registrations**, select **Add existing registration**.
2. Choose the region and the registration.
3. Enter the details Commenda asks for, such as the **Filing frequency** and the **Effective start date for tax account**.
4. Enter your portal credentials, or skip this step if the registration doesn't need them.
5. Select **Add registration**.

## Request a new registration

1. In **Registrations**, select **Request new registration**.
2. Choose the region and follow the steps.

You can also start a registration from [Exposure Tracking](/indirect-tax/exposure).

Some registrations need another registration first. Commenda shows **Prerequisite registration** when that applies.

## Update a registration

Select a registration to open it. From there you can:

- Select **Edit** to change the **Registration details**.
- Select **Update credentials** so Commenda can sign in to the tax portal and file for you.
- Select **Change remittance account** to set the bank account used to pay the tax.
- Select **Archive registration** when you no longer use it.
