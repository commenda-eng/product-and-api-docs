---
title: "Set up Global Indirect Tax"
description: "Turn on Global Indirect Tax for an entity and choose how Commenda gets your sales data."
---

Global Indirect Tax tracks sales tax, VAT, and GST for an entity. Once it's on, Commenda checks your sales against each jurisdiction's registration threshold and shows where you owe tax.

## Turn it on for an entity

1. In the sidebar, open **Global Indirect Tax**.
2. Choose the entity in the entity selector at the top of the page.
3. Select **Get started**.

Commenda opens **Exposure Tracking** for that entity. Repeat these steps for each entity that sells to customers.

## Choose how you send sales data

Commenda needs your transactions to work out exposure and prepare filings. Tell it where they come from.

1. In **Global Indirect Tax**, select **Settings**.
2. Open **Data**.
3. Choose an upload method:
   - **Manual upload**: you upload files yourself.
   - **Manual + integration**: Commenda syncs from a connected system, and you can also upload files.
   - **Integration only**: Commenda syncs from a connected system.

To connect a system, see [Connect your systems](/settings/integrations).

## Review your other settings

**Settings** in Global Indirect Tax has these sections:

| Section | What you set there |
| --- | --- |
| **Access and email handling** | Portal credentials and the email address tax portals use to reach you. |
| **Money and remittance** | The remittance bank account Commenda uses to pay tax. |
| **Data** | How you send transactions to Commenda. |
| **Integrations** | Which connected systems Commenda uses for tax. |
| **Tax logic** | The **Default product tax code** for products without one. |
| **Customer exemptions** | How Commenda handles exemption certificate emails. |
| **Developer credentials** | Your **API key** and **Corporation ID**. Only company admins can see them. |
