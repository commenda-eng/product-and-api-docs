The **Compliance calendar** gives you a unified view of every filing obligation across all your entities and jurisdictions, including compliance items and sales tax filings.

## Accessing the calendar

Click **Compliance Calendar** in the sidebar to open it.

## What you'll see

The calendar displays all compliance items and sales tax filings organized by due date. For each item, you can see:

- **Entity name** — which entity the obligation belongs to
- **Filing type** — what needs to be filed (annual report, tax return, sales tax filing, etc.)
- **Jurisdiction** — where the filing is due (including state for US filings)
- **Due date** — when it must be submitted
- **Category** — the obligation category (for example, indirect tax, corporate income tax, annual report)
- **Frequency** — how often the obligation recurs (monthly, quarterly, annually, etc.)
- **Status** — current progress derived from the underlying filing or compliance item (upcoming, not filed, under filing, filed, overdue, etc.)

The calendar combines two sources of obligations:

- **Compliance items** — entity-level obligations such as annual reports, corporate income tax filings, and other recurring compliance tasks generated from Commenda's obligation rules.
- **Sales tax filings** — indirect tax filings generated from your sales tax registrations.

## Filtering and sorting

You can filter the calendar by:

- **Entity** — view obligations for one or more specific entities
- **Category** — narrow the calendar to specific obligation categories (for example, only indirect tax or only annual reports)
- **Date range** — focus on a specific time period (up to 92 days at a time)
- **Status** — show only items that are not yet filed, or only completed items

The calendar provides both a **summary view** (counts of obligations per date) for quickly identifying busy periods, and a **detailed view** that lists every obligation due on a given day.

## Taking action

From the compliance calendar, you can:

- **Update status** — mark a filing as under filing, filed, or completed
- **Create a service request** — get professional help with a filing by creating a service request
- **View details** — click any item to see its full details, history, and attached documents
- **Request an extension** — if available, file for an extension on the deadline

<Tip>
Check your compliance calendar weekly to stay ahead of upcoming deadlines. Items approaching their due date will be highlighted.
</Tip>
