The **Ownership** tab lets you track who owns each entity and who controls it. Commenda records both **shareholders** (individuals or corporate entities holding equity) and **ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs)** — the natural persons who ultimately own or control the entity, as required by KYC and beneficial ownership reporting rules in most jurisdictions.

UBO qualification is gated by **significant control**: a person is recorded as a UBO when they are explicitly marked as having significant control of the entity, either through their own shareholding or through a corporate parent that has been propagated down. Crossing the jurisdiction's UBO threshold by ownership percentage alone does not automatically qualify a person — you (or your team) make the qualification call by toggling significant control.

<Note>
The redesigned Ownership experience is rolling out progressively. If you do not yet see the features described below, your workspace is still on the legacy cap table view — your account team will let you know when the new experience is enabled.
</Note>

## Viewing ownership

1. Go to **Entities** and select an entity.
2. Click the **Ownership** tab.

The Ownership tab is organized into three sections:

- **Shareholders** — every individual and corporate entity that holds equity in the entity, with their ownership percentage, relationship type, and stock class.
- **Ultimate beneficial owners** — the natural persons who own or control the entity, either directly or indirectly through corporate shareholders. UBOs are computed automatically based on the shareholding tree and your jurisdiction's UBO threshold.
- **Recent documents** — recently uploaded entity documents (incorporation certificates, share certificates, register extracts, etc.).

The header shows the total ownership recorded against the entity. Commenda enforces a **100% ownership budget** — you cannot add or edit a shareholder if the resulting total would exceed 100%.

## Adding a shareholder

Click **Add shareholder** and choose whether the new shareholder is an individual or another entity in your workspace.

### Individual shareholder

For an individual, provide:

- **First and last name**
- **Date of birth**
- **Nationality**
- **Ownership percentage** (0–100)
- **Relationship type** — Founder, Investor, Employee, Board member, Advisor, or Other
- **Stock class** (optional) — pick an existing class, or stage a new class inline (see [Stock classes](#stock-classes))
- **Significant control** (optional) — mark the person as having significant control of the entity

If you toggle **Significant control** on, the individual is recorded as a beneficial owner of the entity at the moment the shareholder is saved.

<Tip>
If the entity already has shareholders without a stock class, the form offers an **Apply this class to all unassigned shareholders** option. Selecting it reassigns every null-class shareholding on the entity to the picked class in the same write — useful when you are recording an entity's first share class and want to backfill the existing register in one step.
</Tip>

### Corporate shareholder

For a corporate shareholder, pick another entity from the same workspace and provide:

- **Ownership percentage** (set to **0** to record a control-only relationship — see [Control-only corporate shareholders](#control-only-corporate-shareholders))
- **Relationship type** — Parent, Investor, or Other
- **Stock class** (optional) — pick an existing class, or stage a new class inline
- **Significant control** (optional) — toggle on if the corporate shareholder exercises significant control over this entity

After saving the corporate shareholder, use **Inherit beneficial owners from parent** on the Ownership tab to propagate one or more of the parent's UBOs down as indirect UBOs of this entity (see [Inheriting UBOs from a corporate parent](#inheriting-ubos-from-a-corporate-parent)).

<Info>
Ownership cycles are rejected at write time. You cannot, for example, make Entity A a shareholder of Entity B if Entity B is already a direct or indirect shareholder of Entity A. Workspaces are also enforced — a corporate shareholder must belong to the same workspace as the entity being edited.
</Info>

## Adding a beneficial owner directly

Some individuals control an entity without holding any equity — for example, a person who can appoint or remove the majority of the board. Use **Add beneficial owner** on the Ownership tab to record them as a UBO without creating a shareholding:

1. Click **Add beneficial owner**.
2. Pick an existing person from the entity's parent company, or enter their first name, last name, date of birth, and nationality to create a new person record.
3. Pick a **Direct control type** — Direct ownership, Indirect ownership, or Other significant control.

The person appears in the **Ultimate beneficial owners** section but not in the **Shareholders** list. The same flow is used to register an existing director as a UBO of an entity they control without holding shares.

### Inheriting UBOs from a corporate parent

When an entity has a corporate shareholder, you can pull one or more of the parent's UBOs down as indirect UBOs of this entity. On the Ownership tab, click **Inherit beneficial owners from parent**, pick the corporate shareholder, then tick which of its UBOs to inherit and pick a [control type](#beneficial-owner-control-types) for each.

Inheritance is an explicit per-write action — selecting nobody is a no-op. Repeating the action later adds more inherited UBOs without disturbing the ones already recorded.

### Control-only corporate shareholders

A corporate entity can also exercise significant control without holding equity — for example, a holding company that has board appointment rights over a sister entity. To record this, add the corporate shareholder with **Ownership percentage** set to **0** and **Significant control** toggled on. The link appears as a dashed edge on the [Corporate structure](/product/entities/corporate-structure) org chart to distinguish it from equity ownership.

## Beneficial owner control types

Each UBO carries a control type per source. Commenda supports the control types most commonly required by global beneficial ownership registers:

- **Direct ownership** — the person directly owns shares
- **Indirect ownership** — the person owns the entity through one or more intermediate corporate shareholders
- **Other significant control** — non-equity control (e.g. board appointment rights, voting trusts)

A UBO can have a **direct anchor** (their own shareholding or a direct registration via **Add beneficial owner**) and one or more **inherited paths** (each path representing a corporate shareholder through which they qualify). Each path carries its own control type — for example, the same person might appear as Direct ownership through their own shareholding and as Indirect ownership through a holding company.

Click any UBO to open their detail panel, where you can review their address, nationality, date of birth, masked SSN (last four digits only), the roles they hold across the entity (director, officer, shareholder, UBO, authorized signatory), and every path that contributes to their UBO status.

You can edit the direct control type independently of any path control types. Editing a path's control type updates only that path — the other paths and the direct anchor are left alone.

## UBO thresholds

The UBO threshold is the minimum ownership percentage at which most jurisdictions require a person to be reported as a beneficial owner. Commenda surfaces the threshold for the entity's **country of incorporation** alongside the shareholder register — for example:

- 25% for most EU countries, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia
- 10% for India, Chile, Argentina, and many Caribbean jurisdictions
- 5% for Colombia, Ghana, Nigeria, and South Africa
- Any positive ownership for Ecuador and Guatemala

Thresholds are surfaced automatically — you do not need to configure them per entity. UBO qualification itself is gated by the **Significant control** toggle, not by the threshold percent.

## Stock classes

If an entity has multiple classes of stock (Common, Preferred Series A, etc.), you can define each class on the entity and assign it to individual shareholdings.

Each stock class has:

- **Class name** (e.g. "Common", "Preferred Series A")
- **Type** (Common or Preferred)
- **Initial shares authorized**

Stock classes are scoped per entity. The **Stock class** picker on every shareholder form lets you pick an existing class or stage a new one inline; the new class is created in the same write as the shareholder, so abandoning the form leaves no orphan class behind.

When you add the entity's first stock class, you can opt to apply the new class to every existing shareholder that does not yet have one — the reassignment runs in the same write as the new shareholder.

Classes that are still attached to a shareholding cannot be deleted — reassign or remove the shareholdings first.

## Non-controlled entities and ownership

If a corporate shareholder is a [non-controlled entity](/product/entities/creating-entities#adding-an-existing-entity), it still appears as a shareholder of the entity below and is included in the [Corporate structure](/product/entities/corporate-structure) org chart. However, Commenda does not walk through a non-controlled parent when computing inherited UBOs — only ownership through entities you control contributes to the indirect-ownership UBO calculation. This keeps reporting focused on the people and entities you actually manage.

## Editing and removing shareholders

Use the row actions on each shareholder to update or remove them.

- **Editing a corporate shareholder** to 0% is allowed only when **Significant control** is on (a [control-only corporate shareholder](#control-only-corporate-shareholders)). Otherwise, remove the corporate shareholder if it no longer holds equity.
- **Removing a shareholder** also removes any UBO paths that only existed because of that shareholding. Other paths and the person's direct anchor are preserved.
- **Beneficial owners** are managed independently of shareholdings. Use **Remove beneficial owner** on the UBO row to clear the direct anchor and every inherited path on this entity in one step. To clear a single inherited path while keeping the rest, edit that path's source corporate shareholder.

## Exporting an ownership summary

Click **Export** on the Ownership tab to download a PDF summary of the entity's ownership and control. The document includes:

- A cover page with entity name, incorporation country, and report date
- The full shareholder register with ownership percentages and stock classes
- All ultimate beneficial owners with their direct control type (where set) and every contributing inherited path
- Snapshot metadata so the report is suitable for sharing with banks, auditors, and regulators

The PDF contains every shareholder and UBO regardless of pagination on screen.

## Org structure view

For companies with multiple related entities, open the **Corporate structure** view to see the full ownership graph as a diagram. See [Corporate structure](/product/entities/corporate-structure) for details.
