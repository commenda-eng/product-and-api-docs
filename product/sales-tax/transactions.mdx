The **Transactions** tab shows all your taxable transactions, which feed into your indirect tax filings and calculations.

## Data sources

Transactions can come from two sources:

1. **Accounting software** — automatically synced from your connected accounting platform (QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Shopify, etc.)
2. **CSV upload** — manually uploaded from a spreadsheet

## Viewing transactions

Go to **Indirect Tax** and select an entity, then click the **Transactions** tab. Each transaction shows:

- **Date** — when the transaction occurred
- **Customer** — who the sale was to
- **Amount** — the transaction amount
- **Tax amount** — the calculated tax
- **Jurisdiction** — where the tax applies
- **Filing status** — whether Commenda included the transaction in a filing

## Uploading transactions

To upload transactions via CSV:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your file">
    Format your CSV with columns for date, customer, amount, and jurisdiction. Commenda provides a template you can download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Go to **Transactions** and click **Upload CSV**. Select your file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    Map your CSV columns to Commenda's transaction fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Review the imported transactions and confirm the upload.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transaction details

Click any transaction to view its full details, including:

- Line items and tax breakdown
- Customer information
- Exemption status (if the customer has a valid exemption certificate)
- Filing assignment

<Tip>
For the most accurate and up-to-date transaction data, connect your accounting software rather than uploading manually.
</Tip>

## Transaction upload method

Each entity has a **transaction upload method** that controls how Commenda expects to receive your transaction data. Your advisor sets this on the entity's indirect tax settings, and it drives the automated reminders described below.

| Method | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Manual upload** | You upload transactions via CSV each filing period. Commenda will send you a monthly reminder with a service request to upload before the next filing deadline. |
| **Manual and integration** | Transactions sync from your connected accounting software, but you may also upload top-up transactions via CSV. Monthly upload reminders are still sent so you can confirm or supplement the synced data. |
| **Integration only** | Transactions sync exclusively from your connected accounting software. No monthly upload reminder is sent. |

If you're not sure which method applies to an entity, check with your Commenda advisor. Switching methods (for example, after connecting a new integration) changes whether the monthly reminder is created for that entity going forward.

## Monthly upload reminder

For entities set to **Manual upload** or **Manual and integration**, Commenda automatically opens an **Upload transactions** service request on the 1st of each month so you have a clear, trackable place to deliver your data ahead of that month's filings.

When the reminder runs, Commenda:

1. Checks each entity's transaction upload method and only proceeds for entities that need a manual upload.
2. Looks up the indirect tax filings due in the current calendar month that haven't been filed yet.
3. Creates one service request per entity, scoped to the current month, and skips creation if a request for the same month already exists.
4. Emails every company admin with the number of filings due, the earliest due date, and a direct link to the service request.

The email recipients see a subject line like `3 filings due this month — upload your transactions` and a single **Upload transactions** call-to-action that opens the service request in Commenda.

<Note>
Only one monthly upload service request is created per entity per month. If your team archives the request and one is needed again in the same month, contact your advisor — the next reminder will be generated on the 1st of the following month.
</Note>

To respond to the reminder, open the service request and follow the steps in [Uploading transactions](#uploading-transactions). Your advisor uses the uploaded data to prepare the [filings](/product/sales-tax/filings) due that month.
