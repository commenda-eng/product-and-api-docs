## Chat with Commenda AI

Ask questions about your entities, filings, and tax in plain language.

1. In the sidebar, select **New chat**.
2. Type your question and send it.

## Schedule an agent

An agent runs instructions on a schedule and sends you the result.

1. In the sidebar, open **Agents**.
2. Select **Create agent**, and enter an **Agent name** and **Instructions**.
3. Choose the **Frequency**: **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
4. Under **Access**, choose who can use the agent and who gets each run.
5. Select **Create agent**. To try it right away, select **Run now**.

## Connect your AI tools with Commenda MCP

Commenda MCP lets your team's AI tools read and write Commenda data. Admins control who has access.

1. Open **Settings** and select **Commenda MCP**.
2. Set the default access for **Admins** and for **Members**.
3. To override the default for one person, find them under **Per-member access** and choose **Read**, **Write**, or **Blocked**.
