---
title: "Connect your systems"
description: "Connect each entity's accounting, payments, and ecommerce systems so Commenda can sync its data."
---

When you connect a system, Commenda syncs its data for the entity you choose. Global Indirect Tax uses that data to track exposure and prepare filings.

## Connect a system

1. Open **Settings** and select **Integrations**.
2. Select **Add integration**.
3. Search for your system and follow the steps to sign in to it.

The connection appears in **Integrations** with its **Entity** and **Sync status**.

## Manage a connection

Select a connection to open it. From there you can:

- See the **Sync status** and the **Scopes** Commenda has.
- Select **Sync updates** to sync now, or **Resync all** to sync everything again.
- Change the **Sync configuration** and see **Settings history**.
- **Disconnect**, **Reconnect**, or **Delete** the connection.

## Use a connection for tax

For Global Indirect Tax, turn on **Calculate tax** for the connection and set the **Tax start date**. Transactions dated before the start date sync but aren't taxed.

For Stripe, paste the **Stripe webhook URL** into your Stripe webhook settings so Stripe transactions can be taxed.
